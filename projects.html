Point-in-time data engine

ChronoFund

SEC EDGAR, XBRL and Bloomberg fundamentals dated to the second the SEC accepted each filing. Four independent layers enforce the cutoff, and any violation fails at parse time rather than months later inside an inflated backtest. Survivorship-free, 65 tests.

Fundamental equity backtest

Regime-Aware Factor Strategy

A walk-forward regime-switching fundamental strategy on ChronoFund data, 2008 to 2026, net of 2/15 fees. Fama-French five-factor attribution reports alpha decay rather than hiding it: 8.37% (t = 2.73) in 2008 to 2017, fading to an insignificant 2.42% over the full period.

See the project
Options engine

PinSight

Infers the risk-neutral density of SPY same-day options and runs a scheduled live paper-trading loop. Every decision is a pure function of an as-of timestamp, and a test proves past decisions cannot change when future data is appended.

See the project
Prediction markets

DriftEdge

Sizes Polymarket and Kalshi binary contracts with Kelly on an empirical-Bayes win probability whose prior is a zero-edge bet, so no evidence means no position. Deterministic replay audits every strategy change; 64 tests with CI.
