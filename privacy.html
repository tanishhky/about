Privacy

Privacy policy for SecondBrain

SecondBrain is a personal automation tool that I, Tanishk Yadav, run on my own computer to organize my own information. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it reads

SecondBrain signs in to my own Google accounts with read-only access, and only to these services:

  • Gmail: job-alert emails in my inbox, to list new job postings.
  • Google Analytics: visit statistics for this website.
  • Google Calendar: my upcoming events, to show deadlines on my own dashboard.
  • Google Health: my own activity, sleep and health measurements, for personal tracking.

It never sends email, changes calendar events, edits analytics settings or writes any data back to Google.

Where the data goes

The data is processed on my computer and stored in local files there. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. I may review it with the AI assistant I use for my own work. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from each Google account's security settings, under third-party connections, and the local files can be deleted.
